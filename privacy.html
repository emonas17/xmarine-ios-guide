Xmarine Android Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

This policy applies to Xmarine for Android (package xmarine.nmea), developed by Emonas Paukste. Xmarine has no user accounts, advertising, or in-app analytics. The developer does not operate a server that receives app usage, location, or boat instrument data.

Location and boat instruments

If you enable Device GPS and grant Android location permission, Xmarine uses device location while the app is in use to display your position. It can also receive position, heading, depth, wind, AIS and other readings from the marine gateway you configure. When you use compatible autopilot features, Xmarine sends commands to that gateway at the address you configure.

Online maps and weather

Depending on the view and layers you use, Xmarine requests imagery from OpenStreetMap, OpenSeaMap, EMODnet Bathymetry, or Observatorio Ambiental Granadilla (OAG). Requests identify the map area being viewed. When you use the wind forecast, Xmarine sends coordinates based on the map view to Open-Meteo. If the map is centered on your position, these requests can reveal where you are. Providers also receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address and handle it under their own policies.

Storage, retention and deletion

Xmarine keeps preferences, gateway settings and saved route names and coordinates in private storage on your device. Map tiles may be cached locally. You can delete saved routes in the app and remove local settings and cached content by clearing app storage in Android settings or uninstalling Xmarine. You can disable Device GPS or revoke location permission in Android settings.

Contact

If you contact support, the developer receives your email address and any information you choose to include. Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle the request. For privacy questions or to request deletion of correspondence, email 17@topgold.lt.
